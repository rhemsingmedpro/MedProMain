How to Send on iPhone (Outlook)

1. Tap Generate PDF above.
2. Your share sheet opens with the PDF ready to send — scroll the app row and tap Outlook.
3. Outlook opens a new email with the PDF already attached.
4. Fill in the recipient, check the attachment is there, and hit Send.
Tip: if Outlook isn't visible in the share sheet row, tap More to find it, or add it as a favorite so it always shows up near the front.
