SettingBattery run time

Duration = (full volume × PSI ÷ 2,000) ÷ flow rate (LPM). A full cylinder is taken as 2,000 PSI. OCD mode multiplies the duration by 3. Estimate only.

Run times are manufacturer or retailer published figures for a new, fully charged battery (typically 20 breaths/min; the Eclipse 5 uses 12). Higher breath rates, age of the battery, temperature and altitude shorten them. ~ = estimated (interpolated or scaled) by this app, not published. Always verify against the device manual.
